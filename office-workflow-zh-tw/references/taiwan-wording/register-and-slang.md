# Register and internet slang in Taiwan (original notes, v0.3.0)

Compiled by this project from general usage and public articles about Taiwanese internet language. All examples are our own. Slang ages fast: treat this list as a guide, not a rulebook.

## 1. Three registers

| Register | Where | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Formal (書面／公文) | official notices, contracts, letters to institutions | 敬請、惠予、茲因、檢送、為荷、請查照; no particles, no emoji; 您 |
| Semi-formal (business) | work email, LINE work groups, customer service | 您好、麻煩您、再請協助、跟您確認、收到、感謝; 您; at most one light particle (喔、囉); emoji rare (🙏) |
| Casual (口語) | friends, social posts, reviews, Dcard/Threads | 你; particles (啦、喔、欸、耶、齁、吧、囉); dropped subjects; no final 。; ～ and line breaks; 1–3 emoji |

**Rules**
- Detect the target register from the user's request and the audience (friends' LINE group = casual; boss = semi-formal; government = formal), then make the whole text consistent.
- The most common AI mistake is mixing registers: 公文 words in a friends' message (親愛的各位朋友…敬請準時出席…一起嗨起來吧), 您 next to a joke, or stacked politeness (非常非常感謝您寶貴的時間並祝您萬事如意).
- Shifting up: 沒辦法→無法, 跟→與, 要→須, 馬上→立即, 但是→不過／然而, 看看→評估; remove particles and emoji.
- Shifting down: the reverse; shorter sentences, 你, one particle where a person would say one.
- Do not go further than asked. "More polite" to a boss means semi-formal, not 公文.

## 2. Casual writing habits

- Particles carry tone, one per clause at most: 啦 (settled, a bit impatient), 喔 (soft heads-up), 欸 (attention, mild surprise), 耶 (delight), 齁 (shared knowledge, "right?"), 吧 (suggestion or guess), 囉 (so then).
  - Example: 這間我吃過了啦，其實沒那麼貴喔。
- Intensifiers: 超、很、蠻、有夠、爆; 超好吃, not 巨好吃／賊好吃 (Mainland).
- Punctuation in chat: final 。 often dropped; ～ softens (好啦～); …… or 。。。 for speechless; doubled ！！ or ？？ is normal in chat but not in brand copy.
- Emoji: 1–3 per message. 😭 can mean "so good I could cry", not only sadness. Many emoji on every line reads like spam.
- Taiwanese Hokkien loanwords (揪團、古錐、俗又大碗、雞婆、機車) fit casual text only, and only if the speaker would plausibly use them. Do not add them to text that did not have them.

## 3. Situational slang

| Situation | Common terms |
|---|---|
| Reviews / shopping | 踩雷、避雷、回購、CP值、開箱、業配、工商、大推、必買、平價、普普 |
| Praise | 讚、神、超強、太強了、好扯 (amazed)、神作 |
| Complaint | 傻眼、無言、崩潰、很雷、白目、扯 |
| Humor | 笑死、哈哈哈、XD (a bit older but alive)、母湯 (jokey "no way") |
| Forums (PTT/Dcard) | 原PO、推、有雷／防雷、鄉民、版友 |
| Work chat | 收到、了解、麻煩了、辛苦了、跟您確認一下、卡關、先這樣 |

**Brand use:** a brand may use at most one light term (CP值、大推、回購) if the source tone allows it. Overused "hype" slang (神級、超狂、爆款、閉眼入、無腦衝) sounds forced. Never add slang to text that has none.

## 4. Mainland internet slang in Taiwan

- **Still marks the text as Mainland; replace for Taiwan readers:** yyds (→ 超神／太強了), 絕絕子 (→ 超讚／絕了), 666 (→ 太強了), 家人們／寶子／集美／老鐵 (→ 大家／朋友們, or drop), 給力 (→ 很讚), 巨／賊 + adj (→ 超 + adj), 打工人 (→ 上班族), 拿捏 (→ 掌握得剛好), 乾飯 (→ 吃飯／開吃), 無語 (→ 無言／傻眼), 價格很香／真香 (→ 價格很漂亮／CP值高), 衝 as "buy now" (→ 快去買／手刀下單).
- **Borrowed and now common in Taiwan, especially online; usually keep:** 破防、內卷、躺平、社死、種草、emo.
- Corporate jargon is covered in `cn-tw-terms.md` (賦能、抓手、閉環、對標、落地).

## 5. Long-form text (blog posts, articles, reports)

- Paragraphs of 2–5 sentences; mobile readers in Taiwan expect short blocks. A one-sentence paragraph for emphasis is fine.
- Remove AI scaffolding: 首先／其次／最後 ladders, 值得一提的是, 綜上所述, a summary paragraph that repeats the intro, bold lead-ins on every bullet, every section ending in a neat moral.
- Keep the author's concrete details and first-person voice; vary sentence length.
- Headings: keep the source's headings. Casual blogs often use plain or playful headings (先說結論、踩雷心得); formal reports keep 一、二、三.
- When editing long text, keep the structure and order; edit within sections rather than rewriting the whole piece.
